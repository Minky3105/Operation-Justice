It is June 14th, 1947. Rancher William Brazel discovers a pile of seemingly out of place junk, like wood, foil, and rubber. Brazel reported the incident to the sheriff of his town on July 7th. Area 51 is a Nevada air force base that keeps the public far far away. CIA agents do supersecret work there. Aliens and their spaceships reside there. As of September 26th, 2025, 4:24 PM, there are 9 crashed flying saucers within the boundaries of Groom Lake.
